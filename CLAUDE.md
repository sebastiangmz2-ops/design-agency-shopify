# The Design Agency — Coastal Pillar House Bible
### Santa Barbara Beach House

*Living reference document. Update this every time a new room, detail, or standard is locked in.*

---

## Exterior & Setting
- Location: Santa Barbara, California coastline (not Hamptons/East Coast)
- Home exterior: white/cream stucco or plaster, dark bronze-black window frames, multi-pane French doors
- Roofline: warm terracotta/wood tone barrel tile, white eave detailing
- Lighting fixtures: black lantern-style wall sconces
- Landscaping: olive trees, ornamental grasses, hydrangeas, coastal shrub, dune-adjacent
- View: Pacific Ocean, dramatic coastal bluff visible in the distance
- Signature time of day: golden hour for exterior/outdoor shots; bright soft diffused daylight for interiors

## Locked Architectural Materials (consistent across all rooms)
- Wall material: white vertical shiplap paneling (narrow tongue-and-groove boards running vertically, painted white) — used house-wide for cottage feel
- Ceiling: light oak wood beams (NOT dark-stained)
- Flooring: wide-plank light oak
- Window frames: white-painted wood, traditional divided-light multi-pane grid, modern-traditional profile, taller floor-adjacent proportions with sheer/linen curtain panels on a simple rod where applicable (CHANGED from original black-frame standard — see Notes/Decisions Log)
- Door hardware: black metal, simple pulls
- Millwork accent color: sage green (used selectively — built-ins, desk nooks, cabinetry)
- Cabinetry detail: woven cane/rattan lattice inserts on sage-green millwork
- Wood tone (furniture): warm light oak throughout

## Standard Photography & Styling Spec
*(append to every Nano Banana prompt)*

> Bright, soft, evenly diffused natural daylight. No harsh shadows, no moody dim tones. Airy, not golden-hour dramatic (unless the room calls for it). Warm-neutral, true-to-life color grading. Eye-level architectural angle, balanced composition, full room in focus, no fisheye/wide-angle warping. Layered but restrained styling — lived-in, not staged-empty, not cluttered. One fresh floral or greenery touch per room. Warm, approachable, elevated mood. Fully photorealistic — realistic material textures, accurate light falloff and shadow, no illustrative/painterly/CGI-render quality, must read as an actual photograph. No people unless requested. No visible logos or existing artwork.
